Who We Are

Al Yafei International LLC

London Cleaning & Maintenance (LCM) began operating as a facilities management company in Muscat in 1992. Over the last three decades, we have risen to become one of the top three FM companies in the Sultanate of Oman.

We are fully equipped with our own extensive in-house resources, including certified technical experts, manpower, logistics vehicles, specialized cleaning machineries, and a solid financial base. We take care of your facility around the clock, allowing you to focus on your core activities in the knowledge that your assets are in expert hands.
